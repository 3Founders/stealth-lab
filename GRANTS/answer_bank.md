## "What do you build?"
> Verified procedural memory for AI agents. We extract procedures from real agent traces, attach provenance + success criteria + lifecycle status, and retrieve them under explicit budget control at decision time — npm plus CI for what agents know how to do.

## "Why now?"
> Both AI labs standardized SKILL.md within eight weeks of each other (Sept/Nov 2025); 40+ clients adopted it via agentskills.io. Portability creates install decisions, and enterprises won't install unverified machine-written procedures. The evidence/lifecycle layer doesn't exist yet — formats don't verify themselves.

## "Traction / progress so far?"
> Working substrate integrated into tau2-bench's banking domain (MCP server). 74.4% token-cost reduction on 15 paired instances with accuracy held flat. Failure classes replicate across independent repos. Public leaderboard attempt running this month; OSS launch next.

## "Who are your competitors? Why will you win?"
> Mem0/Zep/Letta store episodic context — facts and conversations, not procedures with provenance and lifecycle. DevRev validated the direction from inside a $1B company (structured memory 94.3% vs fetch-RAG 63.6% at 4.4× fewer tokens). Nobody does execution-verified procedures. Our moat compounds: every execution adds evidence a copy of our code doesn't have.

## "Isn't this just RAG over logs?"
> RAG fetches text — no success criteria, no provenance, no execution-based promotion, no lifecycle. Fetch-RAG is the baseline everyone abandons at scale (DevRev: 63.6% vs structured 94.3%).

## "Your accuracy is flat — why should anyone believe efficiency alone sells?"
> Correct, and we disclose it first. Efficiency pays today — agent budgets are a board-level pain point. Accuracy gains come from closing the write-side learning loop, our next milestone. We'd rather own the honest baseline than inflate it.
