# Institute ecosystem → B2B clients without equity: first-party research (2026-10-27)

5 Exa searches, ~35 sources scanned; no page-fetches needed. No outreach, no accounts, no forms.
**Summary (≤1 page):**
- The institutes are **introduction engines, not client engines.** Nothing at SINE/STEM/E-Cell hands us a B2B devtools client. Their buyer value = warm access to alumni who are engineering leaders, plus "industry connection" facilitation that historically serves deep-tech manufacturing (Intel/SAP/ONGC at SINE, Boeing/Kirloskar at IISc S12/S13) — poor fit for StealthLab.
- **The "no equity" ask conflicts with the money doors, not the client doors.** SINE's published terms are **1.5% equity (virtual) / ~3% (physical) + revenue share up to a 6% combined cap** [S2][S6] ⚠ (SINE's own slide deck says single-digit equity 1.5–5%, rev share 1.5–3% [S1] — variants agree it is NOT equity-free). STEM Cell historically took **4–10%** [S11] ⚠. Equity-free doors: STEM EIR (validation, no company needed) [S9], IoE Innovation Grant ₹6–9L to students [S4], E-Cell competitions, IITBAA rooms, chapter meets.
- **Best client-ROI sequence:** IITBAA/department warm paths → Bengaluru agentic rooms (already in `CLIENT_FINDING.md` §3) → E-Summit'26 Dec 12–13 as a scouting room (Premium Zone = corporates) → SINE conversation for IoE/SIDBI/SISFS money — only after [DEC] deciding we accept the 1.5–3% toll.
- **Avoid:** pitching incubators for clients; paying Expo stalls (audience is students/investors, not eng leaders); assuming STEM Cell incubates a software tool — its mandate is "science at the core" [S12] ⚠; I-Summit'26 registration (closed Oct 24).

## 2. IISc track

### 2.1 STEM Cell / FSID
- Open to all: "incubation services are open to all and are not restricted to IISc alumni only"; rolling applications to `stem.cell@fsid-iisc.in` [S9] (H) — matches `GTM_STRATEGY.md` §3.
- Fit caveat verified harder this pass: STEM incubates **deep science at the core**; SID "does not incubate pure-play" non-science ventures; Murali: incubatees are mostly PhD scientists [S12] (M). A coding-agent verification layer is a weak candidate for *incubation*, whatever "open to all" says.
- Equity: Murali (2020): **"We take 4–10 percent equity"** [S11] ⚠ vs page's "up to ₹1Cr seed through grants and equity-related funding" (no % stated) [S9]. If Anuj's IISc path goes near incubation, negotiate/confirm equity first — **this is the single biggest equity risk on the IISc side**.
- Useful equity-free piece: **EIR program validates ideas "without the overhead of setting up a company"** and produces an incubation-grade proposal [S9] (H) — an un-incorporated, equity-free IISC-side door.
- Scale: ~94 incubatees (Apr 2025) [S13]; ~30 active, 80 over 10 yrs (2023) [S12].
- **Alumni network is an explicit offering**: "Connect to IISc alumni" on the official offering list [S9] (H) — i.e., incubator membership is one way to buy *access*, but the same access via Anuj's own cohort is free.

### 2.2 SID (industry interface) / CORE Labs / Pravriddhi
- SID is IISc's industry-facing arm; incubation divisions STEM + TIME²; Indian Bank "IND SpringBoard" ₹50Cr MoU (2021 ⚠) for MSMEs/startups incubated at SID; the bank chose institutes because "institutes take care of technical evaluation" [S10] (M, old).
- FSID industry-collaboration vehicles: **CORE Labs** (collaborative research platform), **Pravriddhi** product accelerator — joint GOI+industry funded products (₹40Cr+₹22Cr of ₹62Cr) with Kirloskar Pneumatic, CastNX etc.; project funds from **Boeing, Honeywell** [S13] (H).

- **Verdict: IISc industrial machinery is hardware/manufacturing-shaped.** For us the IISc value is (a) Anuj's cohort/department access to Bengaluru engineering leaders, (b) EIR, (c) credibility by association. Not the corporate portfolio.

## 4. Honest ranking of "ecosystem → B2B client" value
1. **Alumni warm-intro mechanics** (IITBAA chapter events + dept/hostel network + DAA lead lists) — the only channel whose buyer density is right; the playbook lives in `docs/research/institute_alumni_playbook_2026-10-27.md`.
2. **Bengaluru agentic rooms** — already verified in `CLIENT_FINDING.md` §3 (Indian AI Community, eChai 17 founders); institute affiliation only adds credibility, does not replace it.
3. **E-Summit'26 Dec 12–13** — largest single gathering where mid-size company founders pass through; go as attendee, pitch nothing beyond Living-Lab-audience questions.
4. **SINE industry-connection facilitation** — real but B2B-misfit (Intel/SAP/ONGC legacy, deep-tech bias).
5. **E-Summit Expo paid stalls / Techfest** — wrong audience.
6. **STEM Cell incubation** — wrong mandate + heaviest toll.
