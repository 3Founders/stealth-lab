**4.1** Each Founder's shares vest over **[4]** years from **[date]**, with a **[1]-year cliff** (nothing vests before one year, then 25% at the cliff and the rest monthly).

## 5. Leaving

- **Good leaver** (death, disability, resignation after a notice period agreed with the others, removal without cause): keeps vested shares.
- **Bad leaver** (serious misconduct, fraud, material breach, breach of confidentiality or the IP assignment, leaving to compete): the Company may buy the vested shares at **[face value / [●]% of fair value]**.
- Fair value is set by an independent valuer chosen by agreement, or by the arbitrator.

## 6. Decisions

- Day-to-day decisions: the Founder with that role.
- **Reserved matters** need all Founders' (or **[a majority of [●]%]**) consent: issuing shares, raising money, selling the Company or its code, changing the business, borrowing over ₹**[●]**, paying a Founder, approving the budget, changing this agreement.
- **Deadlock:** talk for **[15]** days, then mediation for **[30]** days, then **[one Founder may offer to buy the other out at a stated price and the other chooses to sell or buy at that price / arbitration]**.

## 7. Intellectual property and confidentiality

Each Founder signs the Deed of Assignment of IP and assigns work for the Business to the Company from now on. Each keeps the Business's non-public information confidential. A Founder must not use the Business's code, data or customers for any other purpose.

## 8. Transfers

A Founder may not sell, pledge or give away shares without the others' consent, except to a family trust or entity they control and that returns the shares if control changes. Other Founders have a **right of first refusal** on any permitted sale. If Founders holding **[●]%** agree to sell the Company, the others must sell on the same terms (**drag-along**); a Founder selling a majority must let others sell on the same terms (**tag-along**).

## 9. After a Founder leaves

For **[12]** months a leaving Founder must not solicit the Company's customers or staff. A ban on competing after leaving is generally not enforceable in India (Section 27, Indian Contract Act, 1872), so this agreement relies on confidentiality, IP assignment and non-solicit, not on a post-exit non-compete. A non-compete during the Founder's time with the Company is allowed.

## 10. Money
