# CONFIDENTIALITY AND INVENTION ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

Between **[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]** (**Company**) and **[NAME]**, of **[ADDRESS]** (**Individual**), dated **[DATE]**. Engagement: **[employee / contractor / intern]**, role **[●]**, starting **[DATE]**.

## 1. Confidential information

The Individual keeps confidential, and uses only for the Company, all non-public information about the Company and its customers: source code, schemas, routing logic, procedure libraries, customer data and repositories, pricing, plans. This lasts during the engagement and for **[5]** years after, and for trade secrets and customer source code for as long as they stay secret. Normal exceptions apply (public, already known, independently developed, legally compelled with notice).

## 2. Work product belongs to the Company

**2.1** The Individual assigns to the Company all intellectual property in work made **in the course of the engagement or using the Company's resources or confidential information**, worldwide and for the full term of the rights ("Work Product"). The assignment states the term and territory because the Copyright Act, 1957 otherwise limits an assignment to five years and India.

**2.2** The Individual tells the Company about each invention, and signs documents the Company needs to register its rights. To the extent the law allows, the Individual waives moral rights.

**2.3** Work that the Individual made **before the engagement, and independently of it,** is listed in Schedule 1 and stays the Individual's. If the Individual puts any of it into the Work Product, the Company gets a perpetual, worldwide, free licence to use and sublicense it.

## 3. Customer code and data

The Individual accesses customer code and data only through the Company's approved accounts and environment, for the task assigned. No copy is kept outside it. No customer content is used to train, tune or evaluate a machine-learning model outside the work for that customer.

## 4. No one else's information

The Individual will not bring or use confidential information or code of a former employer, a university or anyone else. The Individual states that doing this job breaches no other agreement.

## 5. Open source and AI tools

The Individual follows the Company's rules on open-source licences and on putting code or customer content into AI tools **[attach policy]**.

## 6. Return of property

On request or on leaving, the Individual returns or deletes all Company and customer material and credentials and confirms in writing.

## 7. After the engagement

The Individual may not solicit the Company's customers or staff for **[12]** months. This agreement does not restrict the Individual from working elsewhere after leaving. A post-employment non-compete is generally not enforceable in India (Section 27, Indian Contract Act, 1872).

## 8. General

Indian law; disputes by sole-arbitrator arbitration in **[city]**. The Company may enforce this by injunction. This agreement survives the engagement. Stamp duty borne by the Company.
