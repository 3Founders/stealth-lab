# Referral and promo codes in India: legal briefing (2026-11-01)

Briefing for a lawyer and a CA. It is **not legal advice**. It comes from one research pass in which the primary texts (India Code, Razorpay's merchant terms) could not be opened, so almost everything is secondary or inference. V = primary text read (none), S = secondary (law-firm or news commentary), I = inference.

## The scheme being assessed

A paying customer can generate a personal referral code. A new buyer who uses it pays the **full list price** (no discount). The referrer earns a small one-off reward, credited as prepaid usage worth about Rs 90 to Rs 150, only **after the referred person's payment succeeded and the refund window has passed**. Credit is spendable only on kell, non-transferable, non-withdrawable.

## Variants and risk (my judgement, I)

| Variant | Risk | Main laws | Most important safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full-price referral, credit reward after the refund window | Low to medium | CCPA endorsement guidelines; GST and s.194R TDS; PCMCS Act only if it drifts | One level only; reward for a completed paid sale, not a signup; capped; referrer discloses the reward |
| Referee discount plus referrer credit | Medium | GST s.15 valuation; dark patterns (price display) | Show the final price including GST before payment; state the discount in advance |
| Plain promo code, no referrer reward | Low | GST valuation; dark patterns and E-Commerce Rules | Terms stating the discount applies at supply, with a clear expiry |
| Cash or UPI reward to the referrer | Medium to high | s.194R / 194H / 194-O TDS; GST; RBI and NPCI rules; PCMCS Act | Don't, until a CA signs off. Keep it credit-only |
| Two-level rewards | **High** | Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act 1978; Direct Selling Rules 2021 (pyramid ban) | **Don't build it** |

## Points by area

1. **Money circulation and pyramid schemes** (S: Prize Chits and Money Circulation Schemes (Banning) Act 1978, ss.2(c), 3, 4, 5; Direct Selling Rules 2021). The usual line (I): illegal when members pay to join and are rewarded mainly for recruiting more paying members. A single-level credit for a completed product sale is on the permitted side. Two-level rewards are the structure regulators examine. No enforcement against SaaS referral codes was found.
   Do: one level; pay only on a completed paid sale; cap rewards per referrer. Don't: reward signups, pay the referrer's referrer, use rank or tier language, promise "earn money".
2. **Consumer law and dark patterns** (S: Consumer Protection Act 2019; Direct Selling Rules 2021 notified 28 Dec 2021; Dark Patterns Guidelines 30 Nov 2023). Customer codes with no authorising contract are probably not direct selling (I); it becomes arguable if referrers recruit, are trained, get targets or recurring commission. Avoid countdowns, fake scarcity, pre-ticked add-ons, conditions revealed after payment.
   Do: show the final payable amount including GST and the code's exact effect. Don't: fake scarcity or hidden conditions.
3. **Endorsements** (S: CCPA Guidelines on Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements 2022). A referrer earning credit has a material connection that must be disclosed clearly; the advertiser carries exposure for false claims.
   Do: put "I earn credit if you use my code" in the share template; ban unsubstantiated savings claims. Don't: pre-write testimonials.
4. **Tax.**
   - GST: a discount known at the time of supply and shown on the invoice reduces taxable value (I, s.15 CGST). The treatment of **referral credit itself was not found** in any circular: open. A Finance Act 2026 change to post-supply discounts is reported from 1 Nov 2026 (S, unverified). Prepaid credit for future services may be an advance (I).
   - Income tax: s.194R (from 1 July 2022; CBDT Circular 12 of 2022; Rs 20,000 per recipient per year; S) may apply to benefits like credit; whether it covers a sole proprietorship was not found. Track cumulative rewards per referrer. The reward is probably taxable to the referrer (I).
   Do: cap total rewards per referrer below Rs 20,000 a year; track them. Don't: pay cash before a CA confirms.
5. **Prepaid credit and RBI** (S: 2021 Master Direction: closed-system instruments need no RBI approval; a 2026 draft says an entity issuing an instrument solely for its own goods and services is not operating a payment system; not confirmed final). Keep credit non-transferable, non-withdrawable, no gifting or cash-out; state validity at issue and what happens on refund.
6. **Data protection** (S: DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025, phased; verifiable parental consent for under-18s about 18 months after notification, and no tracking or targeting of children). Tell a referrer only "someone used your code" and the credit amount, never a name or email. Don't run the scheme for known minors without an adult sign-up. Don't send invitations from kell's systems; let referrers share their own link.
7. **Payments** (not verified): Razorpay's merchant terms could not be read. Ask Razorpay support in writing whether prepaid credit with referral is acceptable. Release rewards only after the refund window and cancel unreleased rewards on refund or chargeback. No UPI payouts until settled.
8. **Programme terms** (I): publish eligibility, trigger (a paid purchase, not a signup), amount, caps, release timing, validity, refund and chargeback effect, fraud clauses, right to amend, "referrer is responsible for own tax", and a grievance contact. Keep rewards fixed (random rewards risk lottery rules). Require an 18+ declaration; agreements with minors are void under the Indian Contract Act.
9. **Fraud:** void rewards for self-referral (same person, device, card, UPI ID), fake accounts and farming; release only after a review check; keep an audit log. Only earned, unspent reward credit can be cancelled, not the customer's own purchased credit.

## Not found at all
GST treatment of referral rewards; ASCI influencer rules; TRAI/TCCCPR text; Razorpay's prohibited-business wording; NPCI cashback guidance; any referral-scheme enforcement.

## Questions for a lawyer and CA
1. Does a customer referral code with a capped, single-level credit reward stay outside the Direct Selling Rules and the PCMCS Act?
2. GST: is referral credit a discount, a supply by the referrer, or neither, and when is prepaid credit "issued" for time of supply?
3. Does s.194R apply to a sole proprietorship, and later to a company; does service credit count as a benefit; what is the rate?
4. Is closed-loop credit with a referral top-up outside the payment-system law under the 2021 direction and the final 2026 one?
5. Can we sell to and run referrals for under-18 students, and what age gate is enough?
6. Does Razorpay's agreement restrict prepaid credit or referral incentives, and what happens to a reward on chargeback?
7. What disclosure wording must referrers use, and what is our liability for their posts?
8. May unspent purchased or referral credit expire, and with what notice?
