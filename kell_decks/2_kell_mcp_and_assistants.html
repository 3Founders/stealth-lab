Proposed pricing plan

Pro, Teams and Enterprise.

Pro

₹999 a month

One person, or a small student team. About half the price of Claude Pro.

  • Connect your assistant or coding agent
  • Read the library and get a routing plan
  • Managed open-model usage included (about $5 of provider cost), or bring your own key
  • A receipt after each task: what kell did and what it cost
  • Public or non-sensitive material only

Billing: monthly, with included usage. Extra usage by prepaid top-up at provider cost plus a published margin.

Status: prepaid top-ups are built and tested, not yet switched on. Monthly plans are planned.

Teams

₹1,199 per seat a month

A lab, department or startup team, three seats or more. About half a Claude Team seat.

  • Everything in Pro
  • Pooled usage across the team
  • Budgets per person and per month, one stop switch
  • Members, invites, usage by person, audit export
  • A shared team library, routed by your team's own results

Billing: per seat, with pooled usage. Extra usage at provider cost plus margin.

Status: admin pages are built but off in production. Seat billing and GST invoices are planned.

Enterprise

Custom, by contract

Private code or compliance needs. Aimed at no more than half the equivalent Claude Enterprise spend.

  • Everything in Teams
  • A zero-retention provider tier under a signed agreement
  • Self-hosted or private-cloud deployment, single sign‑on
  • A private library and private benchmark suites on your own tasks
  • Security review, data-processing agreement, support terms

Billing: an annual contract: seats plus usage, quoted per organisation.

Status: planned. None of these enterprise items is available today.

Proposed prices in rupees, before GST. Open models let us price at about half of the equivalent Claude plan; we confirm the numbers after a joint pilot measures cost per solved task. Claude figures are approximate list prices in India.
