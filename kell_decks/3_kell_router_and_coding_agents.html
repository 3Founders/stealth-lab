The kell router · October 2026

What kell is

Procedural memory, used for routing.

Memory is the asset

Goals, claims, procedures and evidence: which way of doing a task worked, under what conditions, with proof.

Routing is how it pays

Each task goes to the cheapest model, harness and tools measured to succeed. Open models take the routine steps.

Surfaces collect the evidence

Assistants, coding agents and the MCP server all report results back, so every run makes the next choice better.

Status: the memory and the routing are built. The improvement over time is the hypothesis a pilot tests.

The shift

The outcome depends on the model, the harness and the tools together.

Same model, different harness

A cheap model driven by kell's step agent solved the same tasks as when driven by Claude Code, for 3 to 4 times less cost.

Same model, different tools

In an independent test, changing the search engine alone moved scores by about 10 points on average.

So we measure the combination

Not a model's reputation. The set a task actually ran on, and whether it passed.

Our replay covered 3 issues and is a direction, not a final number.

The framework

Goal, claim, procedure, evidence, and now the harness.

1

Goal

What is to be done, with the context that decides how.

2

Claims

What must hold before (assumptions) and after (guarantees). The goal's status is computed from evidence on them.

3

Procedure

Steps, each bound to how it runs: a model, a tool, a harness.

4

Evidence

Pass or fail pinned to the exact procedure version and configuration.

5

Harness

Proposed: part of the step binding and the unit, next to the model and the tools.

Status: framework built. Harness and tools as first-class parts of a measured unit: proposed.

kell · 6

Benchmarking with rigour

Measure before we route on it.

1

Preregister

Write the acceptance rules and the bar before the run.

2

Hold out

Score on tasks the router never saw, with hidden tests.

3

Block leaks

Replays have no network, and a fetched upstream fix counts as not solved.

4

Count noise

Report intervals and repeat runs. A repeated Sonnet run flipped 12 of 82 outcomes.

5

Cost it all

Include the test writer and the reviewer, not just the answer.

Status: these practices are in use on code. The same discipline carries to other task families.

From benchmark to routing

Every result becomes evidence the router can use.

1

Observe

A run becomes an observation on a goal.

2

Roll up

Evidence on specific goals adds to the broader goal above them.

3

Rank

Per kind of work, rank configurations by success and cost.

4

Plan

Choose the cheapest sequence that meets the reliability target.

5

Adapt

A new model is placed from its card on day one, then corrected by its runs.

Status: roll-up and day-one ranking are built. Per-category validation is in progress.

Pricing plan

Pro, Teams and Enterprise.

Pro

₹999 a month

One person, or a small student team. About half the price of Claude Pro.

  • Connect your assistant or coding agent
  • Read the library and get a routing plan
  • Managed open-model usage included (about $5 of provider cost), or bring your own key
  • A receipt after each task: what kell did and what it cost
  • Public or non-sensitive material only

Billing: monthly, with included usage. Extra usage by prepaid top-up at provider cost plus a published margin.

Status: prepaid top-ups are built and tested, not yet switched on. Monthly plans are planned.

Teams

₹1,199 per seat a month

A lab, department or startup team, three seats or more. About half a Claude Team seat.

  • Everything in Pro
  • Pooled usage across the team
  • Budgets per person and per month, one stop switch
  • Members, invites, usage by person, audit export
  • Routing from your team's own measured results

Billing: per seat, with pooled usage. Extra usage at provider cost plus margin.

Status: admin pages are built but off in production. Seat billing and GST invoices are planned.

Enterprise

Custom, by contract

Private code or compliance needs. Aimed at no more than half the equivalent Claude Enterprise spend.

  • Everything in Teams
  • A zero-retention provider tier under a signed agreement
  • Self-hosted or private cloud, single sign‑on
  • Private benchmark suites on your own tasks, feeding your own routing
  • Security review, data-processing agreement, support terms

Billing: an annual contract: seats plus usage, quoted per organisation.

Status: planned. None of these enterprise items is available today.

Prices in rupees, before GST. Priced at about half of the equivalent Claude plan, which is possible because the routine steps run on open models. Claude figures are approximate list prices in India. Extra usage is billed at provider cost plus a published margin.
